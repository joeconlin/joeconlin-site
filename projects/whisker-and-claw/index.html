Whisker & Claw

A browser game where you run a cat-only vet clinic like a tabletop RPG, and a d20 decides how cleanly your medicine goes.

Status · Live

Play it ↗

View as Markdown ↗

Whisker & Claw is a browser game about running a cat-only vet clinic, played like a tabletop RPG. You take a history, examine the cat, order tests, read the bloodwork, make the diagnosis, and treat. Knowing the medicine is on you. A d20 roll decides how cleanly it goes.

Exams turn into a wrangle: keep the cat cooperative before its stress maxes out, using a purrito wrap, a gentle hold, a slow blink, or treat bribes. There are 23 conditions across five tiers, from ear mites to FIP, with realistic feline lab ranges and a short teaching note at the end of each case.

Play solo, or hot-seat with up to four players and AI teammates covering the other roles. It saves in your browser.

Cases are simplified for play, not veterinary advice.
